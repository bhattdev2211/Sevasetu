Community Assistance & FAQ

Help & Guidelines

Find answers to common questions about food safety, voluntary donor privacy, and transparent fund management.

🚨
Life-Threatening Emergency Contacts:

For immediate clinical emergencies or critical ICU blood bank needs, call 108 (National Emergency Ambulance) or 112 immediately. SevaSetu is a community volunteer platform and does not replace emergency clinical hospital services.

🍲 How does FoodShare ensure food safety and quality?

Donors must state preparation time, storage conditions, and a strict pickup deadline (typically within 4 to 6 hours of cooking). Volunteers are instructed to visually inspect containers and verify freshness before distributing meals to shelter homes.

🍲 Can I edit or cancel a food donation after posting?

Yes. Donors can edit quantities, deadlines, and contact information, or cancel active listings directly from the FoodShare page if the food has already been collected through other channels.

🩸 Why are blood donor phone numbers masked?

To protect our voluntary donors from unsolicited commercial spam, phone numbers are masked by default. Requesters must submit their name and hospital case reason through a consent-controlled workflow, which generates an auditable record before contact details are revealed.

🩸 Who is eligible to register as a voluntary blood donor?

Healthy adults aged 18 to 65 weighing over 45 kg, with no recent history of bloodborne illnesses or major surgeries in the last 6 months. Male donors can donate every 3 months; female donors every 4 months.

🛡️ How is the CommunityFunds balance computed?

The balance is calculated directly from persisted transactions: Total Verified Inflow (Income) - Total Documented Relief Disbursements (Expenses). Negative amounts are strictly rejected by the backend.

🛡️ Can anyone record relief expenditures?

While any community member or supporter can contribute funds, only verified group administrators and treasurers (e.g., NGO Organizers or verified stewards) are authorized to log expenditures with invoices and receipt numbers.
