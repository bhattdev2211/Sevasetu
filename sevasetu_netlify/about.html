Our Mission & Purpose

Building Bridges of Compassion & Solidarity

"Seva" means selfless community service. "Setu" means bridge. SevaSetu connects people who have surplus resources with neighbors who need urgent help.

🍲

Zero Food Waste

Bridging the tragic gap between evening banquet surplus and nighttime urban hunger. We connect caterers and restaurants directly with local shelter homes and night kitchens.

🩸

Emergency Blood Access

Creating a trusted voluntary donor network where donor privacy is safeguarded by strict consent controls, and emergency hospital cases get matched quickly.

🛡️

Radical Financial Transparency

Every rupee donated to community disaster or relief drives is publicly accounted for in our open digital ledger with itemized receipts, ensuring zero leakage.

The SevaSetu Story

Across Gujarat's growing cities — Rajkot, Ahmedabad, Surat, and Vadodara — hundreds of generous individuals, caterers, and voluntary donors want to help, but fragmented WhatsApp groups, unverified forwards, and opaque charities make coordination difficult and stressful.

SevaSetu was built to provide a clean, dignified, open technology platform: real-time listings with verified freshness deadlines, privacy-first blood donor discovery, and an auditable community fund where every contributor can see exactly where their rupee made a difference.
