How SevaSetu Works

Simple 4-step workflow connecting those who can give with those who need help.

01

Join or Switch Persona

Sign up as a volunteer, donor, or organizer, or test immediately using pre-configured verified community personas.

02

Publish or Discover

Post surplus food meals, broadcast emergency blood requirements, or contribute to transparent relief drives.

03

Coordinate with Consent

Claim food pickups with estimated arrival times and access blood donor contacts through an auditable consent workflow.

04

Track Public Impact

Every rescue and expense is recorded in real time on public ledger dashboards with exportable CSV audit trails.

Trust & Community Guidelines

Built on privacy, verification, and zero financial opacity.

SevaSetu is built with rigorous ethical guidelines: medical emergency blood requests direct to 108/112 for clinical emergencies, voluntary donor contact numbers are masked behind auditable inquiry logs, and community financial ledgers cannot be corrupted.
